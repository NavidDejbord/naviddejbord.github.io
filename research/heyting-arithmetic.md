# Σ₁-Provability Logic of Heyting Arithmetic

*Master's thesis · Sharif University of Technology · 2024*

**Supervisor:** Mohammad Ardeshir  
**Adviser:** Mojtaba Mojtahedi

My master's thesis concerns the Σ₁-provability logic of Heyting Arithmetic. The work brings together tools from arithmetic, intuitionistic modal logic, and the study of provability principles.

The accompanying English exposition emphasizes the assumptions, logico-mathematical tools, intermediate results, and connections needed to understand the relevant proofs. Topics discussed include arithmetical interpretations and approximation techniques arising in intuitionistic provability logic.
