## Summary

This expository thesis studies the Σ₁-provability logic of Heyting Arithmetic (HA). In the relevant arithmetical interpretation, propositional variables are replaced by Σ₁ sentences and the modal operator □ is interpreted using formal provability in HA.

The main characterization discussed in the thesis is that of Ardeshir and Mojtahedi (2018), who axiomatized the logic, denoted iHσ, and established its arithmetical soundness, completeness, and decidability. The thesis develops the mathematical background and presents the proof-oriented connections between intuitionistic modal logic, arithmetic theories, realizability, NNIL/TNNIL approximation techniques, and Kripke-model constructions.

The exposition gives particular attention to Solovay-style functions, the transformation of finite modal countermodels into arithmetical counterinterpretations, and the reduction of decidability to an effective modal approximation. Its aim is to explain the characterization and its mathematical dependencies; it is not a claim of an independent proof of the original 2018 result.
