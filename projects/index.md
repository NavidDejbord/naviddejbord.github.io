# Projects

This section will contain mathematical and computational projects, including work in formal proof assistants where appropriate.

Formalization projects using Lean, Agda, or Coq can be documented here with links to source code, statements of results, and explanations of the underlying mathematics.

Projects under development will be identified as such; no unfinished work is presented as completed research.
