\section{Research Profile}
My research interests are in mathematical logic, particularly proof theory and provability logic. My previous work includes the provability logic of Peano Arithmetic and the ($\Sigma_1$)-provability logic of Heyting Arithmetic. More recently, I have been independently studying type theory and homotopy type theory, with the aim of developing further research in these areas. I am also open to related directions in logic, foundations of mathematics, and theoretical computer science.

\section{Research Projects}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \cvrow{\href{https://naviddejbord.github.io/research/masters-thesis}{\textbf{$\Sigma_1$-Provability Logic of Heyting Arithmetic}} \textit{(Masters's Thesis)}}{2023--2024}\\
  Supervised by Mohammad Ardeshir, Advised by Mojtaba Mojtahedi\\
  \textit{\small{Studied the characterization of the $\Sigma_1$-provability logic of Heyting arithmetic, including its axiomatization, soundness, completeness, and decidability. Examined the role of realizability, NNIL/TNNIL approximation algorithms, and Solovay-style Kripke-model constructions in the completeness proof.}}

  \item \cvrow{\href{https://naviddejbord.github.io/research/bachelors-thesis}{\textbf{Provability Logic of Peano Arithmetic}} \textit{(Bachelors's Thesis)}}{2020--2021}\\
  Supervised by Mohammad Ardeshir\\
  \textit{Studied the formalization of provability in primitive recursive arithmetic, including Gödel numbering, the diagonal lemma, and Löb's theorem. Examined Gödel–Löb logic and the arithmetical completeness results of Solovay and Visser, including the case of $\Sigma_1$ substitutions.}
\end{itemize}
