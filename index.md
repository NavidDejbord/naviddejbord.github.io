<h1>Navid Dejbord</h1>
<p class="tagline">MATHEMATICAL LOGIC · PROOF THEORY · PROVABILITY LOGIC · TYPE THEORY</p>

<p class="lead">I studied mathematics at Sharif University of Technology, where I completed an M.Sc. in mathematical logic in 2024. My research interests concern mathematical logic, particularly proof theory and provability logic. My academic theses concern arithmetical provability in primitive recursive arithmetic and the Σ₁-provability logic of Heyting Arithmetic.</p>
<p>I have more recently been studying type theory and homotopy type theory independently. I am also interested in related questions in foundations of mathematics and theoretical computer science.</p>
